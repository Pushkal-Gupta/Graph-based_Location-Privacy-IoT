Cover Letter

To: The Editor-in-Chief
    IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing

Subject: Submission of "MIRAGE: Optimal, Road-Graph-Native Location Obfuscation
         at City Scale for IoT Smart Cities"

Dear Editor,

Please find enclosed our manuscript, "MIRAGE: Optimal, Road-Graph-Native Location
Obfuscation at City Scale for IoT Smart Cities," which we submit for consideration
as a regular paper in the IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing.

Continuous location reporting is fundamental to IoT smart cities, yet the many
obfuscation mechanisms proposed to protect it are scored on incompatible,
family-specific scales, and none of the classical optimal-mechanism results has
ever been instantiated on a real road network at city scale. Our manuscript
addresses both gaps.

The main contributions are:

1. An adversary-grounded, threat-model-aware privacy metric measured in metres as
   the expected error of an optimal Bayesian adversary, instantiated under both a
   snapshot adversary and a Viterbi trajectory adversary with a Markov mobility
   prior. We show the two adversaries rank mechanisms differently, so a single
   privacy scale is inadequate.

2. MIRAGE, a road-graph-native optimal obfuscation mechanism that scales to a real
   city by solving one small linear program per density-adaptive local region,
   reducing an intractable O(|V|^2) program to |V| small ones. We validate that the
   decomposition is within 1.5% of the global optimum in the operationally relevant
   regime.

3. The first measurement of the "price of heuristics" on real OpenStreetMap
   networks of two cities (Beijing and Porto) under three mobility datasets
   (GeoLife, T-Drive, Porto taxi): at matched utility, differential privacy and
   k-anonymity forfeit 5-22% of achievable privacy, with statistical significance
   established by a paired region-level bootstrap (p < 0.001).

4. An explanation of the cross-city variation in terms of prior heterogeneity, and
   a reported negative result: snapshot-optimality does not imply
   trajectory-optimality, because the scalability partition leaks region
   membership. This isolates a well-defined open problem.

The complete framework, code, processed graphs, and figure-generation scripts are
released for reproducibility at:
https://github.com/Pushkal-Gupta/Graph-based-Location-Privacy-IoT
